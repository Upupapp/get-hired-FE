Privacy & Data Use

CVCOACH uses your CV to give you applicant-side feedback only.

  • Your CV file is uploaded to secure cloud storage and linked only to your own applicant profile -- other applicants and employers cannot access it directly.
  • Only your name/filename and the file itself are stored; no analysis, scoring, or AI processing happens on it yet, since that engine doesn't exist in this version of GetHired.
  • Uploading a new CV keeps your previous version instead of erasing it -- see Versions / History to view or permanently delete a prior version.
  • Your CV is never made public or shared with third-party AI services.
