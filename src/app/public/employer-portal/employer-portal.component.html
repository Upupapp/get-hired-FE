Easy Job Posting

Explainable Match Signals

Shortlist with more context, not a black box

GetHired match signals are designed as decision-support indicators. They help employers understand possible fit using available applicant and job information while keeping the hiring decision with the employer.

  • Explainable compatibility indicators
  • Structured profile and job information
  • Helpful review context, not hidden rankings
  • Human hiring decision remains central
  • No automatic hiring decisions or rejections
